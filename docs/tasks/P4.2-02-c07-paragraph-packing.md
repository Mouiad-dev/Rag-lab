## Goal
A pure C07 plugin packs whole paragraphs into chunks within a code-point budget, with an explicit, configurable overflow behavior for paragraphs larger than the budget, and exact source offsets.

## Read only these files
- `packages/chunking/src/rag_foundry_chunking/boundaries.py`, `sentence.py`, `__init__.py`
- `packages/techniques/src/rag_foundry_techniques/kinds.py` (`ChunkInput.blocks`, `TextBlock`)
- `tests/chunking/test_sentence.py` (test style)

## Change
- Create `packages/chunking/src/rag_foundry_chunking/paragraph.py`:
  - `ParagraphConfig` (frozen, `extra="forbid"`): `chunk_size: int = 1200` (32..8000), `overlap_paragraphs: int = 0` (0..2), `overflow: Literal["sentences", "hard_cut"] = "sentences"`, `use_blocks: bool = True`.
  - `paragraph_ranges(doc, use_blocks) -> list[tuple[int, int]]` in `boundaries.py` or `paragraph.py`: if `use_blocks` and `doc.blocks` is non-empty, each block's trimmed range is one paragraph, and a block that contains a blank line is split further at blank lines; text outside any block is split at blank lines too. Otherwise split `doc.text` at blank lines (`\n`, only non-newline whitespace, `\n`). Ranges trimmed, ascending, non-overlapping, covering every non-whitespace code point.
  - `ParagraphChunker`: manifest `C07`, `PluginKind.CHUNKER`, `1.0.0`, `Fidelity.ADAPTATION`, `config_model=ParagraphConfig`. Paragraphs within budget are units at level 0. An oversized paragraph: `overflow="sentences"` turns it into `sentence_ranges` (default abbreviations) units at level 1, with oversized sentences further split into words (level 2) then `safe_cut_ranges` (level 3); `overflow="hard_cut"` turns it into `safe_cut_ranges` units at level 3. Overflow units of one paragraph are never overlapped with each other; `overlap_paragraphs` applies only between level-0 units. Then `boundaries.pack`.
- Edit `__init__.py`: register `ParagraphChunker`; export `ParagraphChunker`, `ParagraphConfig`. Do not change other lines.
- Create `tests/chunking/test_paragraph.py` (`\u` escapes):
  - Three short paragraphs that fit: one chunk; with a small size: one paragraph per chunk and no paragraph is cut (each chunk equals a whole trimmed paragraph).
  - An oversized Arabic paragraph with `؟` and `.` endings and `،` inside: `overflow="sentences"` gives chunks that end at sentence ends (level 1), never after `،`; `overflow="hard_cut"` gives level 3 and no cut before a haraka.
  - Blocks: a `ChunkInput` with two `TextBlock`s on one line (no blank line between) gives two paragraphs; `use_blocks=False` gives one.
  - Mixed Arabic/English NFC paragraphs: exact offsets (`text[start:end]` equals the expected paragraph string).
  - `overlap_paragraphs=1`: adjacent chunks share exactly one whole paragraph; starts/ends strictly increase.
  - Coverage, bounds and determinism; registry `validate_config("C07", {}) == ParagraphConfig()`; unknown key and bad `overflow` rejected.
