## Goal
- Split an answer into claims (sentences). For every claim, report:
  - citations to markers that were never shown (`unknown_marker`);
  - citations to markers that were shown but not given to the model (`not_provided`);
  - uncited claims;
  - a stated **lexical** support rule.
- The lexical rule is not entailment. The code, docstrings and statuses must say `lexical`.

## Change
- Edit `packages/answering/pyproject.toml`: add `"rag-foundry-chunking"` to dependencies. The orchestrator runs `uv lock`; only workspace entries change.
- Create `packages/answering/src/rag_foundry_answering/claims.py`. Models are frozen with `extra="forbid"`:
  - `ClaimFlag(code: unknown_marker|not_provided|constraint_missing|low_overlap, value: str)`;
  - `ClaimCheck(index, start, end, text, markers, status: supported_lexical|unsupported_lexical|uncited|invalid_citation, flags, overlap: float | None)`;
  - `CitationReport(rule="lexical-v1", claims, supported, unsupported, uncited, invalid, ok)`.
- `validate_citations(answer, *, provided: Mapping[str, str], known: Collection[str], min_overlap: float = 0.5) -> CitationReport`. A `min_overlap` outside (0, 1] raises ValueError. The rule goes in the docstring:
  1. **Claims.** `sentence_ranges(answer, 0, len(answer))`. A segment that is empty after `strip_markers` (marker only) adds its markers to the previous claim and extends its `end`. A leading marker-only segment goes to the next claim instead. Claim text = `strip_markers(segment).strip()`.
  2. **Markers.** `parse_markers` on the segment(s). A marker not in `known` gets the flag `unknown_marker`. A marker in `known` but not in `provided` gets `not_provided`.
  3. **Status order:**
     - any unknown or not-provided flag gives `invalid_citation` (overlap None);
     - else no markers gives `uncited` (overlap None);
     - else the lexical checks run against the cited provided texts.
  4. **Lexical checks.**
     - **Constraints:** each `evidence_constraints(claim)` value missing from the union over the cited texts gives the flag `constraint_missing`.
     - **Overlap:** `overlap = |content_terms(claim) & union content_terms(cited)| / |content_terms(claim)|`. It is None when the claim has no terms. `overlap < min_overlap` gives the flag `low_overlap` with value `f"{overlap:.2f}"`.
     - Any flag gives `unsupported_lexical`; otherwise the status is `supported_lexical`.
  5. `ok` is True only when there is at least 1 claim and every claim is `supported_lexical`.
- Export `validate_citations` and `CitationReport` from the answering `__init__.py`.
- Create `tests/answering/test_claims.py` with:
  - `S1 = "Tolls are 5 USD per car. This does not apply to bicycles."`
  - `S2 = "The bridge opened on 3 March 2019."`
  - `provided={"S1": S1, "S2": S2}`, `known={"S1","S2","S3"}`

  Expected:
  1. `A = "Tolls are 5 USD per car [S1]. The bridge opened in 2019 [S2]. Bicycles pay 7 USD [S1]. It has a cafe. The cafe sells tea [S3]. Hours are long [S9]."` gives 6 claims, statuses `[supported_lexical, supported_lexical, unsupported_lexical, uncited, invalid_citation, invalid_citation]`, and counts 2/1/1/2 with `ok` False. Also:
     - claim 0 spans (0,29) with overlap 1.0;
     - claim 2 has flags `[(constraint_missing,"7"), (constraint_missing,"7 usd")]` and overlap `pytest.approx(2/3)`;
     - claim 4 has flag `(not_provided,"S3")` and claim 5 has `(unknown_marker,"S9")`.
  2. `"Tolls are 5 USD per car. [S1]"` gives 1 claim spanning (0,29), with markers ("S1",) and `supported_lexical`.
  3. `"Ferries are free [S2]."` gives `unsupported_lexical` with flag `(low_overlap,"0.00")`.
  4. Losing case, asserted and commented: `"Tolls apply to bicycles [S1]."` is `supported_lexical` with overlap 1.0, although S1 says it does **not** apply. The lexical rule misses a dropped negation.
  5. AR. Provided S1 = `"رسوم الجسر ٥ دولار. لا تنطبق الرسوم على الدراجات."`:
     - `"رسوم الجسر 5 دولار [S1]."` gives `supported_lexical` (the ASCII 5 matches the Arabic-Indic 5);
     - the same claim with `٧` instead of `5` gives `unsupported_lexical` with flags `(constraint_missing,"7")` and `(constraint_missing,"7 دولار")`.
  6. A marker group: `"Tolls are 5 USD per car [S1, S2]."` gives markers ("S1","S2") and `supported_lexical`.
  7. Edge cases:
     - `min_overlap=0` raises ValueError;
     - `""` gives 0 claims and `ok` False;
     - two calls give equal `model_dump_json()`.
