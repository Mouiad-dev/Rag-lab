## Goal
HitRate@k, Recall@k, MRR and nDCG@k, computed from source-span relevance with explicit denominators. Unanswerable cases are handled explicitly. Every required cohort always appears, even when n=0. This follows the union-coverage rule of `rag_foundry_chunking.comparison` (P4.5/P5.2) but stands alone.

## Change
Create `src/rag_foundry_evaluation/metrics.py`.

**`EvalHit`** (frozen, `extra="forbid"`): `doc_key: str | None` (None means an unbound view), `start`, `end`, `rank`, `score: float`, `text_sha256`.

**`CaseMetrics`** (frozen, `extra="forbid"`):
- `answerable`
- `found_ranks: tuple[int | None, ...]` (one per labelled span, in label order)
- `first_relevant_rank`
- `hit_rate`, `recall`, `ndcg`: `dict[int, float]` or None
- `rr` (float or None)
- `returned_hits`
- `top1_score` (None if there are no hits)

**`case_metrics(case, hits, *, ks=(1,3,5), threshold=0.8) -> CaseMetrics`**
- `ks` must be sorted, unique and in 1..50. `K = max(ks)`. Hit ranks must be exactly 1..n in list order. Otherwise raise `EvaluationError("invalid_ranking")`.
- `cov_k(r)`: the length of `r` covered by the union of the top-k hits with `doc_key == r.doc_key`, divided by `(r.end - r.start)`. `found_rank(r)` is the smallest `k <= K` with `cov_k(r) >= threshold`, or None.
- `HitRate@k = 1.0` if any span has `found_rank <= k`, else `0.0`.
- `Recall@k = (#spans with found_rank <= k) / |R|`.
- `RR = 1 / min(found_rank)`, or 0.0 when nothing is found within K. This is MRR@K at the mean level.
- nDCG@k:
  - Gain at rank i is `2**g - 1`, where g is the max grade among spans whose `found_rank == i`, else 0. Spans found at the same rank as a higher-grade span are not credited again, so nDCG stays at most 1. They still count in Recall.
  - Consequence (state this in the docstring): when one chunk covers several labelled spans, nDCG cannot reach 1, even though Recall is 1. In Case B below, nDCG@5 is 0.826.
  - `DCG@k = sum over i in 1..k of gain_i / log2(i + 1)`.
  - `IDCG@k` uses the grades sorted in descending order at ranks `1..min(|R|, k)`.
  - `nDCG@k = DCG@k / IDCG@k`.
- Unanswerable case: all metric fields are None. `returned_hits` and `top1_score` are still set.
- Do not round inside the per-case metrics.

**`aggregate(rows, ks) -> tuple[GroupSummary, ...]`**
- `rows` is a sequence of `(EvalCase, CaseMetrics, error: bool)`.
- Output order: splits in the order dev, validation, held_out (only splits that are present). Within each split, groups in this order: `all`, `language:en`, `language:ar`, `language:mixed`, `tag:identifier`, `tag:paraphrase`, `tag:diacritics`, `tag:table`, `tag:ocr`, `unanswerable`. Every group is always emitted, even with n=0.
- `GroupSummary` fields:
  - `split`, `group`
  - `n_cases`, `n_answerable` (the denominator), `n_unanswerable`, `n_errors`
  - `hit_rate`, `recall`, `ndcg`: `{k: mean}`, each rounded to 6 places
  - `mrr` (rounded to 6 places)
  - The means are None when `n_answerable == 0`. Means are computed unrounded, then rounded.

Export the new names.

## Tests (`tests/evaluation/test_metrics.py`)
Expected values below are hand-computed, ks=(1,3,5), threshold 0.8. Use `pytest.approx(v, abs=1e-6)`.

- **Case A**: spans r1 `d[0,10)` grade 2, r2 `d[20,30)` grade 1. Hits:
  1. `other[0,10)` (same offsets, other doc)
  2. `d[0,5)`
  3. `d[5,12)`
  4. `d[20,30)`
  5. `d[40,50)`

  Expected:
  - `found_ranks == (3, 4)`
  - HitRate `{1:0, 3:1, 5:1}`
  - Recall `{1:0, 3:0.5, 5:1}` (HitRate is not Recall at k=3)
  - `rr == 1/3`
  - nDCG@1 `0`, nDCG@3 `0.413117`, nDCG@5 `0.531731`
- **Case B**: the same two spans and one hit `d[0,30)` at rank 1.
  - Recall@1 `1.0`, `rr == 1.0`
  - nDCG@1 `1.0`, nDCG@3 `0.826235`, nDCG@5 `0.826235` (r2 is not credited twice)
- **Threshold boundary**: span `d[0,10)` with hit `d[0,8)` is found at rank 1. With hit `d[0,7)` it is not found.
- **Case U** (unanswerable) with 2 hits: metrics None, `returned_hits == 2`, `top1_score` equals hit 1's score.
- **`aggregate([A, B, U])`** in split dev:
  - `all`: `n_answerable == 2`, `n_unanswerable == 1`, `mrr == 0.666667`, HitRate@1 `0.5`, Recall@3 `0.75`, nDCG@3 `0.619676`, nDCG@5 `0.678983`
  - `tag:table` has `n_cases == 0` and `mrr is None`
  - an error row adds 1 to `n_errors` and stays in the denominator
- Ranks `[1, 3]` raise `invalid_ranking`.
